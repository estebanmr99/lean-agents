# Audit 2026-11

Read-only audit of lean-agents at v0.6.0 plus session 22 (2026-11-06). Nothing here is fixed in the session 22 PR: every medium or higher row becomes a labeled issue, listed in [ROADMAP](ROADMAP.md) once filed.

- **Load assumed:** one developer running the installer on their own machine, sometimes behind a corporate proxy; CI with real binaries; outside contributors opening PRs.
- **Method:** the `/ponytail-audit` skill on `scripts/`, `install.sh` and `install.ps1`; four read-only exploration passes (README EN/ES, security and workflows, network and privacy, repo health). Rows marked *spot-checked* were re-read by the author of this file; the rest come from one pass and need a quick confirm when the fix is started.
- **Severity:** high = wrong claim to users or code run without a pin or consent; medium = real gap with a clear fix; low = polish.
- **Not checked:** upstream telemetry of Serena, Playwright, Context Mode, codeburn, autoskills, ponytail and the Bun installer; whether `rtk init` calls the network; whether branch protection is applied on GitHub; `tests/` and most of `scripts/lib/adapters/` beyond the cited lines; `lean.mjs` beyond the cited lines.

Paths are relative to the repo root.
